Save the King – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Save the King does not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not collect personal information ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google, which serves the ads.

This policy applies to the Save the King mobile game for iPhone ("the Game"), published by Emirhan Yılmaz ("we", "us"). By using the Game you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.

  • Game progress: the highest level you have reached is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the Game.

  • No analytics: the Game does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.

  • No device permissions: the Game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds. No ads are shown while you play a round.

To show and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect the following from your device (Google's data disclosure):

DataWhat Google uses it for
IP address (approximate location)Estimating your general region
Device identifiers (an app- or developer-scoped ID)Advertising and measurement
Ads you have seen and tappedAdvertising and analytics
App interactions, such as app launchesImproving ad performance

The Game does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not give Google your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy; see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your choices about ads

In regions where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. Ads are only loaded after that choice.

  • Change your choice: where consent applies, a PRIVACY button on the main menu reopens the consent message at any time.

  • Personalized ads: if you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which are based on the general context rather than on you.

  • Device settings: you can also limit ad tracking in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center.

Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention, security and your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep, share or sell. Your saved progress stays on your device until you delete the Game.

  • Data held by Google: Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data, or to opt out of its sale or sharing for advertising. For data processed by Google, use the choices above or contact Google; for anything else, contact us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions or requests about your privacy: email emirhanra@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
